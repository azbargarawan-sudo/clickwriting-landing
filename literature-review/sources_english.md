# English verified OA pool (peer-reviewed, full access)

1. Ayanwale, M. A., Sanusi, I. T., Adelana, O. P., Aruleba, K. D., & Oyelere, S. S. (2022). Teachers' readiness and intention to teach artificial intelligence in schools. Computers and Education: Artificial Intelligence, 3, 100099. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.caeai.2022.100099
   - 368 Nigerian in-service teachers; intention to teach AI predicted by relevance, attitude, confidence, AI for social good; AI anxiety and readiness not significant.
2. Al Darayseh, A. (2023). Acceptance of artificial intelligence in teaching science: Science teachers' perspective. Computers and Education: Artificial Intelligence, 4, 100132. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.caeai.2023.100132
   - TAM-based SEM; science teachers positive attitudes and high intention, driven by ease of use and self-efficacy.
3. Chiu, T. K. F., Xia, Q., Zhou, X., Chai, C. S., & Cheng, M. (2023). Systematic literature review on opportunities, challenges, and future research recommendations of artificial intelligence in education. Computers and Education: Artificial Intelligence, 4, 100118. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.caeai.2022.100118
   - Systematic review: 13 AI roles in 4 domains (learning, teaching, assessment, administration), 7 outcomes, 10 challenges.
4. Chan, C. K. Y., & Hu, W. (2023). Students' voices on generative AI: Perceptions, benefits, and challenges in higher education. International Journal of Educational Technology in Higher Education, 20, Article 43. https://doi.org/10.1186/s41239-023-00411-8
   - 399 HK students; positive attitudes to GenAI, personalized support, concerns re accuracy, privacy, ethics, personal development.
5. Menon, D., & Shilpa, K. (2023). "Chatting with ChatGPT": Analyzing the factors influencing users' intention to use the Open AI's ChatGPT using the UTAUT model. Heliyon, 9(11), e20962. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.heliyon.2023.e20962
   - 32 interviews; UTAUT four factors + perceived interactivity + privacy concerns explain ChatGPT acceptance.
6. Ogurlu, U., & Mossholder, J. (2023). The perception of ChatGPT among educators: Preliminary findings. Research in Social Sciences and Technology, 8(4), 196-215. https://doi.org/10.46303/ressat.2023.39
   - Educator survey post-ChatGPT release; mixed perceptions: integrity concerns + instructional benefits.
7. Chai, C. S., Lin, P.-Y., Jong, M. S.-Y., Dai, Y., Chiu, T. K. F., & Qin, J. (2021). Perceptions of and behavioral intentions towards learning artificial intelligence in primary school students. Educational Technology & Society, 24(3), 89-101. http://index.j-ets.net/Published/24_3/ETS_24_3_07.pdf
   - 682 primary students; self-efficacy, AI readiness, AI for social good, AI literacy shape intention to learn AI.
8. Zawacki-Richter, O., Marín, V. I., Bond, M., & Gouverneur, F. (2019). Systematic review of research on artificial intelligence applications in higher education - where are the educators? International Journal of Educational Technology in Higher Education, 16, Article 39. https://doi.org/10.1186/s41239-019-0171-0
   - Review of 146 publications 2007-2018; absence of educator perspectives, dominance of CS/STEM authors.
9. Hatlevik, I. K. R., & Hatlevik, O. E. (2018). Examining the relationship between teachers' ICT self-efficacy for educational purposes, collegial collaboration, lack of facilitation and the use of ICT in teaching practice. Frontiers in Psychology, 9, Article 935. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2018.00935
   - 1,158 Norwegian teachers; ICT self-efficacy and collegial collaboration positively associated with ICT use in teaching.
10. Wang, K., Ruan, Q., Zhang, X., Fu, C., & Duan, B. (2024). Pre-service teachers' GenAI anxiety, technology self-efficacy, and TPACK: Their structural relations with behavioral intention to design GenAI-assisted teaching. Behavioral Sciences, 14(5), 373. https://doi.org/10.3390/bs14050373
    - 606 pre-service teachers, UTAUT SEM; GenAI anxiety, social influence, performance expectancy predict intention; technology self-efficacy and TPACK in model.
11. Ning, Y., Zhang, C., Xu, B., Zhou, Y., & Wijaya, T. T. (2024). Teachers' AI-TPACK: Exploring the relationship between knowledge elements. Sustainability, 16(3), 978. https://doi.org/10.3390/su16030978
    - AI-TPACK framework validated; interrelations among AI-tech, pedagogical, content knowledge.
