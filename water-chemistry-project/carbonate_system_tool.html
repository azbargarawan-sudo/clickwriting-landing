Model. Equilibrium constants K₁, K₂, KH, Ksp,calcite and Kw are temperature-dependent (Plummer & Busenberg 1982). Activity coefficients from the Davies equation with a Debye–Hückel A-parameter computed from the temperature-dependent dielectric constant of water. pH is solved by bisection on the alkalinity balance and reported on the activity scale. SI = log₁₀(aCa·aCO₃ / Ksp). * The seawater preset (I = 0.7 M) is outside strict Davies validity and is illustrative only.
